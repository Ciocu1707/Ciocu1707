# Pe așteptare (On Hold): treatment

Style: Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon. 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 50 s. Romanian dialogue (Microsoft neural voices Emil and Alina through edge-tts), Romanian subtitles burned in plus `.srt`. No storyboard stop.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **Parallel worlds errand.** The duo jumps through worlds to find a human operator. Rejected: too close to the style's demo (a genius tumbling through worlds on an errand).
2. **Tech-support split screen.** Split screen between the kitchen and a call centre in another dimension. Rejected: two sets halve the size of every face, and the joke lives on the faces.
3. **One world, many clocks (chosen).** The duo never leaves the kitchen. The device skips *time*, not space, and each skip is worse while the queue position barely moves. The world changes palette with every skip, so the medium's "we jumped" grammar still works, but the set and framing stay constant. That makes the absurd differences pop, and the repeated framing is itself a gag.

Opening from the topic: **mid-frustration**. The first image is the phone receiver in extreme close-up, playing the hold message. Ending: **the callback loop**. The operator finally answers, Ilinca forgets why she called, she is put back on hold, and the duo jump again.

## 2. Logline and arc

A nervous woman stuck on hold lets her deadpan husband skip the wait with a time-skipping gadget. Decades pass, the queue barely moves, and when someone finally answers she has forgotten why she called.

- **Setup (0–11 s):** a hold message, "position 47"; Ilinca is fed up; Doru pulls out the Sărito-matic.
- **Turn (11–33 s):** two skips. After one week she is position 46. After forty years they are both old, a fuzzy creature lives in the corner, and she is position 45.
- **Reversal (33–46 s):** a third skip into the suspiciously normal year 3000. An operator answers. Dead air. "...I forgot why I called." She is transferred and put back on hold at position 47.
- **Button (46–50 s):** Doru: "Sărim?" ("Skip?") Ilinca: "Sărim." ("Skip.") Portal, cut to black.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:** the adult-swim-era sci-fi sitcom grammar (reaction close-ups, dead air, petty goals with absurd means) and the "Groundhog repetition" pacing of single-set sitcom cold opens.
- **Don't take:** any character design, portal look, colour combination, catchphrase or name. Our cast: Doru (tall, bald, a thick black moustache, a colander-helmet with two antennas, a mustard knitted vest) and Ilinca (short, a huge purple curly-hair cloud, a pink quilted bathrobe, always holding a corded landline receiver). The device is a TV remote with a lumpy cyan portal ring.

## 5. Beat sheet

Locked after the voice durations are measured (`timeline.js` is the source of truth): hook 0–3.4 s, setup to 11.4 s, two skips to 32 s, silence 34–37 s, reversal to 46 s, button to 50 s.

## 6. Cue map

- **Hold music (diegetic, through the receiver):** a cheesy major-key "on hold" loop. Square-wave melody, telephone band-pass at 300–3400 Hz, 112 bpm. It plays under shots 1–2, in every world from the phone, and again at 42.6 s.
- **Theme (score):** a theremin lead with analog bass and a square arpeggio in D minor, 112 bpm, under the device reveal and every jump.
- **World cues:** +1 week = toy-piano lullaby; +40 years = elevator bossa (Karplus–Strong nylon guitar); year 3000 = **nothing**, only a fridge hum.
- **Silences:** the music hard-cuts at 32.0 s (year 3000) and at 38.6 s before "Am uitat…". The first sound after the silence is the click of the transfer, then the hold music.

## 8. Subtitles and titles

Baloo 2 ExtraBold, white with a black outline, bottom centre, with a speaker pill in the character's colour (Doru mustard, Ilinca pink, Telefon grey). A world tag in VT323 typed in, top-left: `ACUM`, `+1 SĂPTĂMÂNĂ`, `+40 ANI`, `ANUL 3000`. The end title "PE AȘTEPTARE" is set in chunky lettering, popped in on 12 fps steps on the last hit. Its palette is cyan on navy, never green slime.
