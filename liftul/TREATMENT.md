# Liftul (episode 3, new cast): treatment

Style: Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon. 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 60 s. Romanian dialogue (edge-tts) and Romanian subtitles. A new cast.

## Cast

- **Tanti Leana**, the lift operator. A tiny, round old lady with a leather aviator cap, goggles and a red uniform jacket with gold buttons. She knits on a stool and works a brass lever. Deadpan, slow, low voice.
- **Mihai**, the intern. Tall and lanky, with a carrot-orange hair tuft, a green hoodie under a tie and a company lanyard. He holds a tray with one steaming coffee. Panicked, fast, high voice.
- The travel device is **the lift itself**. Its lever knob and the light in the door gap glow **magenta**, the only glowing colour in the film.

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **One long trip** to a single strange floor. Rejected: one world, too few visuals.
2. **A chase through the building.** Rejected: walking is not this style's comedy.
3. **The doors as a frame (chosen).** Constant framing inside the lift: Mihai on the left, Leana on the right, the doors centre-back. Every "ding" opens the doors on a new world. Sometimes the world **comes in** (water stays outside as a wall, a giant cat's paw reaches in, space pulls the coffee out). It is a rule of three → montage → the suspiciously familiar floor 9: the same lift, with themselves inside.

## 2. Arc

- **Setup:** Mihai slides in sideways as the doors close: floor 9, the boss will kill him if the coffee gets cold. Leana: "Ține-te bine." She pulls the lever, and the floor dial spins.
- **Floor 3, underwater:** a wall of water, a fish in a tie and a shark with a mug. "Ăsta nu e etajul nouă." "Nu. E trei."
- **Floor 7, giant cat:** one huge eye fills the doorway, then a paw reaches in for the coffee. Mihai: "Pisică! Pisică! PISICĂ!" Leana bonks the paw with a knitting needle: "Șapte."
- **Floor 13, space:** zero gravity. The coffee floats out as a blob and Mihai slurps it back into the cup. "Cafeaua plutește!" "Treisprezece."
- **Montage, 1 s each:** "Cinci." (everything is cheese, mice in suits), "Minus doi." (a jungle, a gorilla receptionist), "Opt." (it rains indoors over the desks).
- **Floor 9:** the doors open on the same lift, with Mihai and Leana inside, staring back. Silence. Mihai: "...Ăștia suntem noi." Leana: "Etajul nouă e liftul. Și eu sunt șefa." "Dumneavoastră?!" She sips: "E rece." Mihai faints, stiff as a plank. Leana: "Ține-te bine." Lever. End.

## 3. Benchmark

Learn the reveal grammar of sitcom elevator gags: the door as a frame, the reaction close-up after each reveal, and silence before the last reveal. Take no characters or designs.

## 4. Shots

| Shot | Framing | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Lift wide, the same every time | Mihai left, Leana right, doors centre | only the world in the doorway changes |
| Reaction close-ups | Mihai panics, Leana deadpan | the laugh lives on the faces |
| Insert: the floor dial | the needle spinning | the time and space jump |
| Floor 9 | slow push into the doorway, the double lift | the cold callback |

## 5. Sound

Each floor gets its own cue that starts on the "ding": underwater bubbles and a dub bass, a cat purr and a meow, a space theremin, a polka for the cheese floor, jungle drums, rain. Lift muzak plays between floors. The music hard-cuts at floor 9: only the hum of the lift is left.
