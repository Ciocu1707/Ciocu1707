# Telecomanda (episode 2): treatment

Style: Sci-Fi Sitcom Toon. 1920×1080, 24 fps, about 60 s. Romanian dialogue (edge-tts, Emil and Alina) and Romanian subtitles. Same cast and engine as episode 1, "Pe așteptare".

## 1. Three candidate structures

1. **A single long jump** to one strange era, played out at length. Rejected: the episode-1 viewer already knows the device. This episode has to escalate.
2. **Doru chases Ilinca through time.** Rejected: action-heavy. The comedy of this style lives in reactions, not chases.
3. **Channel surfing through time (chosen).** Ilinca, bored on the sofa, takes the Sărito-matic for the TV remote and zaps through eras. The **sofa, TV and Ilinca stay in the same framing**; only the world behind them changes. Doru reappears in every era in that era's costume, mid-sentence, and is cut off by her "Nu." It is a rule of three → machine gun, then the suspiciously familiar world (their own year-3000 selves from episode 1), then the reversal.

The roles are reversed from episode 1: Ilinca is the deadpan one now, and Doru panics.

## 2. Logline and arc

Ilinca mistakes Doru's time remote for the TV remote and channel-surfs through history looking for her soap opera. Each time, Doru pays the price.

- **Setup (0–7 s):** static on the TV, Ilinca bored. From off screen, Doru warns her flatly not to touch "that remote". She presses it.
- **Turn (7–34 s):**
  - **Dinosaurs:** a dinosaur licks Doru's colander. "Nu prinde nimic." ... "Nu mă interesează." Zap.
  - **Ancient Rome:** Doru in a toga, "Ave, Ilin—". "Nu." Zap.
  - **Machine gun of four 1-second eras:** an ice block, disco 1978, pirates, outer space. The same "Nu." every time.
- **The familiar world (34–44 s):** the year 3000 from episode 1. Their ancient selves are still on hold. Ancient Ilinca turns slowly: "Și voi sunteți pe așteptare?" Silence. "...Nu." Zap.
- **Reversal and button (44–60 s):** back home, Doru finally grabs the remote. The TV now works and shows Ilinca's soap opera. A melodramatic man: "Esmeralda... eu sunt tatăl tău." Ilinca: "Ooo! Asta e telenovela mea!" Doru looks at the screen and says, deadpan: "Nu." Click. Cut to the end card.

## 3. Benchmark

- **Learn from:** the adult-swim-era sitcom grammar (a running gag tightened into a machine gun, the hard cut on a repeated word, a callback to an earlier episode as the suspiciously normal world) and channel-surfing gags in TV comedy, where the joke is the cut itself.
- **Don't take:** characters, designs, portal looks, catchphrases or melodies from any show. All of it is our own cast from episode 1.

## 4. Shot list (constant framing is the signature)

| # | Framing | Camera | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | insert: TV static | slow push | hook: the boredom itself |
| B | close-up Ilinca on the sofa, static light | static | her deadpan; the reach for the remote |
| C | **the sofa wide**: sofa centre, TV on the left, Doru on the right | locked off, the same in every era | only the world and Doru's costume change: the machine gun reads instantly |
| D | reaction close-up of Doru in each slow era | hard cut | his panic against her calm |
| E | insert: thumb on the glowing button | hard cut | every zap gets the same insert, shorter each time |
| F | year 3000: two-shot of young versus ancient selves | very slow push | the cold callback silence |
| G | home: the TV shows the soap opera | insert | the payoff picture |
| H | Doru close-up: "Nu." | hard cut to the end card | the reversal button |

## 5. Sound

- The **TV static** hiss is the bed at home. Each era gets its own one-second genre cue, cut on the zap: jungle drums, a Roman fanfare, wind, disco, a sea shanty accordion, a space theremin.
- The **"Nu."** is the same clip every time. The music hard-cuts on it.
- The **year 3000** has no music, only the fridge hum. The first sound after the silence is "...Nu."
- The **soap opera** gets swelling tremolo strings and a dramatic sting, cut dead on Doru's "Nu."
